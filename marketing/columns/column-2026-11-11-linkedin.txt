Two headlines this year look like opposites.

In September, Vietnam's education minister told a National Assembly committee that schools are short of 104,500 teachers against staffing norms.

In Korea, the Ministry of Education expects about 298,178 children to start first grade in 2026, the first time the number has fallen below 300,000. Planned hiring of new public elementary teachers was cut by about 27%.

Too few teachers in one place. Too few children in the other. But in both, what decides whether a child learns is the same: a trained, supported adult with time to notice them.

So at Oriel Foundation, our first questions to any future partner will be about teachers, not buildings. We plan steady support partners can count on, and in Korea, we want to find the children who are easiest to miss.

We are still preparing to launch in January 2027 and are not taking donations.
