GivingTuesday is on 1 December. Oriel Foundation will not send an appeal.

We are still preparing to register as a nonprofit, and we will not accept a single gift until we do. So we looked at what the day actually counts.

On GivingTuesday 2025, Americans gave a record $4.0 billion. But of 38.1 million people who took part, 19.1 million gave money. 11.1 million volunteered, and 20.9 million spoke up for a cause. About half took part without opening a wallet.

In Korea, 26.1% of people said they donated in the past year (Statistics Korea, 2025), still below 29.9% in 2015. A new foundation cannot assume trust. It has to show the work first.

So on 1 December we will publish how every future gift will be recorded, invite people to follow the work, and ask for advice and introductions, not money.
