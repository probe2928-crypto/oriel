Oriel Foundation's website went up four weeks ago. This week we wrote our first progress report, and we tried to include the parts a new foundation is tempted to leave out.

What we have done: our rules for partners, child safeguarding and transparency are public. Six issues of Notes. A first field journal entry. A volunteer trips page, with a first five-day trip to the Philippines planned this winter alongside a local volunteer organisation. No orphanage visits, ever.

Why the Philippines: the World Bank says 91% of 10-year-olds there cannot read and understand an age-appropriate text. A short trip will not change that. Meeting the people already working on it is where we start.

What has not happened yet: three emails to local organisations, no replies so far. No volunteer applications recorded. No registration. An empty ledger, because there is nothing to record.
